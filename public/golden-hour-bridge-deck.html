Reality: what the data says

The incident
Survival

The drop is the notification gap: bystander CPR in India runs 1.3–9.8% (vs a 62% global target), and Indian OHCA (cardiac arrest) survival sits under 10%.

The real life-saving metric isn't just clinical CPR. It's closing the notification gap, providing immediate presence to escalate care, and stopping bleeding for the 480,000+ road accident victims India records annually.
