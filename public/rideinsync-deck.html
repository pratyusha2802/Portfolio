Feature deep dive

01

Group operational state

Raw GPS gives you dots. Dots need decoding at 90 kilometres an hour, which is exactly when nobody can decode anything.

RideInSync resolves every rider into one of four plain states, updated live for the lead and the sweep.

Live for lead and sweepRider set stopped, rejoining, leaving
Speaker notes

Start with the thing a map cannot say. RideInSync turns raw GPS into four plain states. Intact, rider behind, rider stopped, location stale. The lead reads a status, not a scatter of dots, and knows in one glance which rider needs a decision right now.

Feature deep dive

02

A shared route on real roads

A pin and a straight line is not a route. It is a suggestion that every rider resolves differently, which is how a group becomes two groups.

RideInSync generates one road following route and gives the same line to everyone on the ride.

One line for the whole groupSplits become visible
Speaker notes

Every rider gets the same line, and that line follows the actual roads. Not a pin and a straight arrow. When the whole group is on one route, a split at a junction becomes obvious instead of invisible, and a rider who leaves the route gets flagged.

Feature deep dive

03

Navigation that reads your speed

City riding needs the next fifty metres. Highway riding needs the next two kilometres. One fixed zoom serves neither.

The map zooms with your speed and stays heading up, so the screen matches what the rider is looking at.

Heading up, alwaysTurn by turn
Speaker notes

Navigation that reads your speed. Crawling through a town, it zooms in for the next turn. Open up on the highway and it pulls back for the sweep ahead. Heading up, always, so the screen matches what the rider is actually looking at.

Feature deep dive

04

The rendezvous route

Groups never start together. Someone is late, someone is twelve kilometres away, and the ride stalls while a pin gets shared on WhatsApp.

RideInSync builds a live route from the scattered rider to the lead, and deletes it the moment they regroup.

Updates as the lead movesSelf clearing
Speaker notes

Groups never start together. The rendezvous route builds a live path from a scattered rider to the lead, updating as the lead keeps moving, and it disappears the moment you are regrouped. No stale route left cluttering the ride.

Feature deep dive

05

One tap, and the group knows

The worst case is a rider down and nobody aware until the next fuel stop. Minutes are the whole game.

SOS fires to every rider on the ride and to the emergency contact, with location attached, so the sweep turns around knowing where to go.

Group plus emergency contactLocation attached
Speaker notes

One tap, and the whole group knows. The SOS goes to every rider on the ride and to the emergency contact, with location attached. The sweep turns around knowing exactly where to go, instead of finding out at the next fuel stop.

Feature deep dive

06

Voice first, because hands are busy

On a motorcycle, a touchscreen is a dangerous interface. Gloves, vibration, and both hands already committed.

Say "sync" and the signal. Hazard, regroup, pit stop, SOS. It goes to the group without a single tap.

Minimal touch while ridingOn device notifications
Speaker notes

Hands stay on the bars. Say sync, hazard, or sync, regroup, or sync, pit stop, and the signal goes to the group. Voice first is not a gimmick on a motorcycle. At a hundred kilometres an hour it is the only input a rider actually has.

Monetisation, freemium plus partnerships

Free for the group. Paid for the rider. Partners pay for the audience.

Free core

  • Ride creation and joining by code or QR
  • Live group status and shared route
  • Voice signals and one tap SOS
  • The whole group has to be on it

Paid tiers

  • Advanced offline and vector maps
  • Longer ride history and larger groups
  • Premium safety. Auto incident SOS, emergency SMS
  • Club and organiser plans

Partners and brands

  • Lubricant and fuel brands (Motul, Shell and peers) placed at planned pit stops
  • Event organisers pay to route riders to the venue
  • Manufacturer and gear advertising to a verified riding audience
  • Sponsored rides and club tie ups

Payments are future scope, not v1. Partner names are targets, not signed deals.
