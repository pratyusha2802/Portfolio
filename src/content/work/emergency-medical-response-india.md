## The problem

Self-directed discovery work, framed around a specific claim: emergency
medical response in India is usually discussed as a transport-speed
problem — get an ambulance there faster. The framing this work starts from is
different — that the pre-ambulance window (the time between an emergency
happening and professional help arriving) is an *ownership gap*, not a speed
gap. Nobody — bystander, family, dispatcher — has a clearly defined role in
that window.
